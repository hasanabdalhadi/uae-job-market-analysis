# 🇦🇪 HJMI — UAE Job Market Intelligence

**HJMI (Hasan Job Market Intelligence)** is a data-driven technology job market intelligence project focused on the **United Arab Emirates**.

The platform analyzes UAE technology job opportunities to provide clear insights into **job roles, in-demand skills, hiring locations, companies, and market trends**.

### 🔗 Live Platform
**[Open HJMI — UAE Job Market Intelligence](https://hjmi-uae.streamlit.app/)**

---

## 🎯 Project Mission

> Turning job market data into clear insights that help people understand where technology opportunities are heading.

HJMI is designed to transform continuously updated job market data into useful and understandable insights for students, technology professionals, and job seekers interested in the UAE technology sector.

---

## 📊 What HJMI Analyzes

- 💼 UAE technology job opportunities
- 🧠 In-demand technical skills
- 🏢 Companies hiring technology professionals
- 📍 Technology jobs across UAE locations
- 📈 Job market trends and patterns
- 🔎 Searchable job market data
- 💰 Available salary information

---

## ⚙️ Automated Data Pipeline

HJMI uses an automated data pipeline to collect and prepare UAE technology job market data.

The workflow includes:

**Job Data → Automated Collection → Data Cleaning → Analysis → HJMI Dashboard**

GitHub Actions automatically runs the data preparation workflow on a scheduled basis, allowing the dataset and dashboard to evolve as the UAE technology job market changes.
